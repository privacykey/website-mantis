about

A tripwire that calls home.

mantis creates unique URLs you can place in files, systems, and websites. When a URL is fetched, you get a signal to investigate. The name comes from the praying mantis: still until something moves.

From a request to an alert

Choose a backend, create a key, and place its URL where an unexpected fetch would matter. Supported document generators and device installers help you place it. The full server records hits and queues notifications; edge forwards them to a webhook.

A hit is evidence of a request, not proof of a person's identity or intent. Readers, preview services, crawlers, proxies, and scanners may fetch links too. File behavior depends on the format and reader. Check compatibility and test your canary before relying on it.

What you can use it for

  • Baited documents, honey-directory bundles, and credential/config artifacts.
  • Supported login, sudo, boot, wake, and network events, with device bundles for several alarms at once.
  • Bulk key creation and managed-device enrollment with create-only credentials.
  • CSS and JS web snippets, NFC/QR URLs, and configured Apple Wallet passes.
  • Notifications and automation through your existing services.

Choose how it runs

Full is a Next.js and Postgres server with a dashboard, key management, history, and delivery retries. Edge is a stateless Cloudflare Worker with self-contained URLs and webhook forwarding. One CLI supports both through different command groups.

Where it fits

Canaries provide another signal alongside access controls and monitoring. They are discoverable, depend on a network fetch, and do not replace prevention or incident investigation. Forward webhooks to your existing tools when you need broader correlation.
