accessibility

Read and use mantis your way.

Plain-language summary

You can use this site with a keyboard. You can change the text size, spacing, and colors. Animation is off unless you turn it on. If a word is new to you, use the glossary. We aim to meet the highest WCAG level, but more testing with assistive technology is still needed.

Reading settings

Use your browser's zoom controls, or enlarge the text here. Reading view uses a simpler font, wider spacing, and one column. Your choices stay in this browser.

Your colors (optional)

Leave both fields blank to use the theme chosen in Menu. To use your own colors, enter a six-digit color code in each field. For example, #ffffff is white and #000000 is black. Choosing a theme in Menu clears these custom colors.

Use the site with a keyboard

  • Press Tab to move forward and Shift + Tab to move back. A visible outline marks your place.
  • The first link, Skip to content, moves past the repeated navigation.
  • Press Enter on links. Press Enter or Space on buttons and expandable sections.
  • Open Menu to reach all pages and color themes. Press Escape to close it. Moving to the next item outside the menu also closes it.
  • Use arrow keys in theme and text-size lists. Use arrow keys to scroll a focused transcript or wide table.
  • The demo accepts text commands. Type help and press Enter for examples. The same main actions are available as buttons.

Choose whether things move

Animation starts off. On the homepage, select Animate demo to allow movement. Clear it to stop the globe. Use Skip animation during a replay to see the whole result at once. When your system requests reduced motion, animation stays off and the demo still works.

Understand the words

Each page has a short summary in plain language. The mantis glossary explains technical words and abbreviations. Links open in the same tab unless you choose otherwise with your browser.

Accessibility status

Our target is WCAG 2.2 Level AAA. WCAG means Web Content Accessibility Guidelines. This target covers this marketing website; the separate documentation site and Mantis software need their own reviews. Automated checks alone cannot prove full conformance.

We check text contrast, page structure, keyboard controls, reading settings, and narrow layouts. Testing with people who use assistive technology is still needed before making a formal AAA conformance claim.

Report an access problem

Report a website accessibility problem on GitHub. Please include the page, what you tried to do, your browser, and any assistive technology you use. Do not include private information. GitHub may ask you to sign in.
